PLAN YOUR ENVIRONMENT

A practical path
to evaluation.

The recordings show a configured research environment. Installation, performance and validation on your infrastructure are separate steps.

  1. Choose the workflows

    List the cases, data types and services you plan to use. Compare them with the documented capability scope.

  2. Check dependencies

    Review hardware, model weights and third-party licenses. Optional providers may require accounts and credentials.

  3. Configure the deployment

    Review storage, service endpoints, permissions and network access for the intended environment.

  4. Validate with your data

    Test accuracy, error cases, data handling and multi-user performance before production use.
